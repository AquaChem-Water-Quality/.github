## What Is AquaChem

AquaChem is a water-chemistry helper for people who already have laboratory or field results and need to see what those numbers say. AquaChem water quality is the table you build first: one row or one station, with the ions you were asked to enter. A student lab, a senior project, or a small environmental office can keep the set small and still do a serious review. The point is to understand measured water, not to dress up an empty sheet.

AquaChem piper diagram and AquaChem stiff plot are the two pictures most classes expect. The piper view shows where a sample sits among major ions. The stiff view shows the same sample as a pattern you can recognize again next week. AquaChem ion balance sits before both pictures. If the charges do not agree, the plot will only repeat a bad analysis. AquaChem sample report is what you keep after the check, so the diagram and the table travel together.

In a longer sitting, the same order stays useful. Enter the sample, read AquaChem ion balance, then look at AquaChem piper diagram and AquaChem stiff plot. Write AquaChem sample report only when the water type is clear enough to explain out loud. AquaChem water quality remains the list you return to when a classmate asks which bottle was the sodium-chloride type. That loop fits home study after a lab day and a small project that has a handful of stations rather than a national network.

## Starting with AquaChem

Prerequisites: A compatible computer, a water analysis you are allowed to use, and permission to run AquaChem.

1. **Prepare:** Copy one real or textbook analysis into a note before you open the sample list.
2. **Enter The Sample:** Build AquaChem water quality with the major ions, pH, and the station name.
3. **Check Charge:** Read AquaChem ion balance and correct a unit or a missing ion before you plot.
4. **See The Type:** Open AquaChem piper diagram and describe the water type in one sentence.
5. **See The Shape:** Open AquaChem stiff plot for the same sample and compare it with a second bottle if you have one.
6. **Keep The Page:** Save AquaChem sample report for your lab book, then repeat the order on the next station.
